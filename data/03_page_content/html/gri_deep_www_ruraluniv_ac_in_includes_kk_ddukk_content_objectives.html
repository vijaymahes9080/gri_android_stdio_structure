Objectives

  • To formulate programmes / courses and create skilled manpower for industry requirements at various levels
  • To set up modular vocational training centers targeted towards empowering underprivileged youth from rural and urban and semi-urban communities, leading to employment and income generating opportunities
  • To empower men/women for socially and economically backward sections and helping them in augmenting their family income by providing meaningful skill trainings enhancing the market access for their goods and produces
  • To develop location specific rural enterprises by promoting rural entrepreneurs with eco-friendly and sustainable technologies that provide clean drinking water, energy access, smokeless cook-stoves, agri-farm support and many more; and
  • To create empowered youth leaders through leadership training programmes in order to ensure ownership, sustainability of project and commitment
