Profile

The Department of Cooperation was established in 1956. It is one of the oldest Departments in Gandhigram Rural Institute. It is the first of its kind in offering Cooperation as an academic discipline at University level in India with the basic objective of developing manpower with updated knowledge and skill in managing, facilitating, organizing, and revitalizing cooperatives and other organizations particularly in rural areas.
